# CLAUDE.md – Dynacorp 2092

Non-commercial Android fan game inspired by Dynatech (1992). Owner: Rocco (GitHub poundah-bit). Talk to him in German; code, identifiers and comments in English; all player-facing text in German.

## Read first

- `docs/konzept.md` – the full game concept (German): original game analysis, goods/facilities/ships, planets, credit, storage, touch UI plan, roadmap, legal notes, and the decisions taken while building Phases 0 and 1. When concept and `src/data/*.json` disagree, the data wins.

## Project rules

- No assets, code, texts or names from the original Dynatech. Planets, companies and values are our own design.
- No combat, sabotage or piracy. Cooperation via the order exchange is the core idea.
- Single player against 1–20 AI companies (chosen before the game, default 10). No split-screen / local multiplayer (decided). Online multiplayer is a maybe-later.
- Music: the soundtrack is by Thamanoidis (Rocco's Suno account name). Every music credit or reference (credits, start screen, settings, docs) names Thamanoidis, never Rocco.
- Target: Android tablet first, then phone. Delivered as PWA (GitHub Pages) and later APK via Capacitor.

## Architecture

- `src/data/*.json` – all game data and balancing parameters. Change numbers here, not in code.
- `src/sim/` – the economy core. Pure TypeScript, no DOM, deterministic per seed (RNG state lives in `World.rng`).
  - `types.ts` world state and data types
  - `data.ts` typed data access, `validateData()`
  - `world.ts` world creation, RNG, lookups, money transfers (`pay`)
  - `actions.ts` every move a company can make; returns `{ ok, value } | { ok: false, error }` with German messages. The UI and the AI must both go through these.
  - `engine.ts` `step(world)` = one game day: bank → facilities → ships → orders → Terra demand → prices → storms → Lieferrouten → AI → bankruptcy → collapse
  - `ai.ts` rule-based AI companies
  - `stories.ts` story events: pure generator (`makeLore`, `pickStory`, `storyStrength`, `validateStories`) over the building blocks in `src/data/stories.json` (20 causes with place rule, effects, ≥ 3 text variants; name pools), limits in `config.json` `stories`; `storiesTick(w)` in `step()` after `ordersTick` (phases, news priority 3, new event every 30–60 days, max 2, planet orders), `storyFactor(w, good, 'terra' | planet)` used by `terraDemands`, `startStory` for tests and the observation page
  - `projects.ts` Großprojekte (player only): `startProject` / `abortProject` / `projectsTick` (the site in Terra's orbit posts orders for its materials, `Demand.project`, cash check; once all material is there a build phase of `projects.buildDays` (200) counts down `ProjectState.buildDaysLeft`, advantage/prestige/quark only after it; `projectProgress` = material and build 0..1), `projectDelivered`, `hasProject(w, c, id)`; data `src/data/projects.json`, numbers `config.json` `projects`. Advantages: dock = free auto repair on Terra, dome = story events announced 7 days early (`StoryState.upcoming`, only then RNG differs), yard = `repairShipOnSite`, city = `facilityLimit` +1, datacenter = `routeLimit` +2, clinic = storm damage halved. `Company.prestige` adds to `companyIndex`
  - `quark.ts` Quark (project variant, decided): `cellOf(good)` from `goods.json` `quark` (industry/supply × weight 1–3 → up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom); every finished Großprojekt unlocks its flavour, `quarkStatus` = unlocked flavours + progress of the running project, `quarkTick` messages at 90 % and on unlocking (once per flavour, `World.quark` holds only `warned`/`reached`; `ensureQuark` for old saves). The quark bar is material/2 + build/2 of the running project; projects from earlier modules (`fromModule`) do not unlock again
  - `campaign.ts` Kampagne (module worlds): `World.campaign` (`world`, `modules: ModuleRecord[]`, `usedNames`; missing = world 1), `closableQuarks` (unlocked here, not produced before), `closeWorld(w, f)` freezes the world into a `ModuleRecord` and returns the next world from `createWorld({ campaign })` – new company with start capital, same difficulty, AI count and story switch, new seed from the old RNG; the closing project stands there as done with `fromModule` (works via `hasProject`, no prestige); after the sixth quark no new world (`campaignComplete`). `campaignValue` = module indices + running world. `worldName` („Welt n · <outermost planet>-Sektor“)
  - `galaxy.ts` planets of a generated module world: `generatePlanets(w, usedNames)` keeps the facility mix of `planets.json`, puts each facility in the radius range of its tier in world 1 (raw inner, final outer), keeps the minimum spacing and keeps mean pair distance, mean distance to Terra and the mean supply line (`galaxyStats().supplyMean`) within `config.campaign.pathTolerance` (10 %) of world 1; names from `src/data/planetnames.json`, never reused. Planet lookups take the world: `planet(w, id)`, `distance(w, a, b)`, `planetsFor(w, type)`, `worldPlanets(w)` (`World.planets` missing = `planets.json`); balancing (`metrics.ts`) measures on world 1
  - `report.ts` balancing report for headless runs
  - `metrics.ts` Phase-2 checks against the concept's target values (amortisation, transport days, loan share, storage share, loss-making planets) plus run statistics
- `src/ui/` – the game (`index.html`): one player vs. 1–20 AI companies (default 10, `AI_DEFAULT` in `play.ts`). Seven tabs (Terra, Karte, Anlagen, Flotte, Börse, Aufträge, Statistik); on a tablet in landscape (≥ 1100 px wide) the star map sits in the side column next to the lists and the Karte tab is hidden.
  - `play.ts` pure player-side logic, no DOM: queries (deliverable demands, waiting transports), combined one-tap moves (`deliver`, `moveTransport`, `carry`, `shipDemand` with a `Choice` of own ship / buy ship / public), `guide()` (the "Nächster Schritt" hint derived from world state) and `fittingOffers`/`newOffers` for the exchange notification. Tests drive the game through this module.
  - `game.ts` start screen, intro sheet, time loop (paused, 1× = 6 s/day, 2×, 4×), click dispatch via `data-act`, sheets, toasts, autosave, save-slot sheets, exchange notification (tab blinks + toast), long press on anything with `data-tip` shows the explanation instead of running it (mouse: hover title)
  - `views.ts` HTML of the list tabs; `data-guide` attributes mark what the guide highlights, `data-tip` holds the long-press explanation
  - `starmap.ts` canvas star map (drag, pinch, wheel, zoom buttons, tap a planet); one element that `game.ts` moves between side column and Karte tab. `mapinfo.ts` the planet panel under it (pure HTML)
  - `chain.ts` chain graph in the Statistik tab: goods as SVG nodes in columns by their longest production path, tap for producers, consumers and market (pure HTML/SVG)
  - `sheets.ts` dialogs (loan, repay, deliver, transport, order goods); `format.ts` German number formatting and labels; `save.ts` one autosave slot in localStorage; `slots.ts` ten save slots in IndexedDB (a late game is ≈ 0.5 MB, too big for ten slots in localStorage)
  - `cues.ts` pure detection of the sound cues of one game day (`cueTracker`): big Terra order for an own good, plant stops producing, ship critical, plant built, ship idle again; `repair` is played on the player's tap. `sound.ts` makes the sounds with the Web Audio API (no sound files: soft sine/triangle notes, low-pass, short generated room echo), settings in localStorage (`dynacorp2092.sound.v1`), at most two cues per day and a cooldown per cue; the menu has a "Musik und Töne" sheet with volume, on/off per cue and a listening test
  - `music.ts` soundtrack by Thamanoidis: three Opus files in `public/music/` (80 kbit/s, ≈ 2.4 MB each, the only sound files in the game), played with an `<audio>` element so they stream; random order, never the same track twice in a row (`nextTrack`), 3 s gap, starts on the first tap, pauses while the app is hidden, skips a track that fails to load. Own settings in localStorage (`dynacorp2092.music.v1`, default on, 40 %), on/off, volume, now playing and „Nächstes Stück“ in the „Musik und Töne“ sheet. Opus does not play on older iPhones/iPads – the game then stays silent
  - Readability (Optik Teil 1): `icons.ts` own line icons (24 grid, stroke 2, `currentColor`) for tabs, cargo types, the 17 facility types and states – `icon(name)` returns inline SVG, the path data is reusable with `Path2D`; `format.ts` `bar()` fill bars (`role="meter"`) and the tone rules `stockTone`, `facilityConditionTone`, `shipConditionTone`, `deadlineTone`; `play.ts` `areaStats()` key figures; `views.ts` `areaHead()` header of Anlagen/Flotte/Börse/Aufträge. Area colours `--area-facilities/-fleet/-market/-orders` colour only frame elements (tab, header, card edge); green/yellow/red stay status only. `npx tsx tools/screens.ts` takes screenshots of the four area tabs (tablet + phone) with a local Chrome into `screens/`
  - Star map look (Optik Teil 2): `planets.ts` generates every planet in code from its id – `LOOKS` per facility type (palette, bands/spots, clouds, craters, lava, night lights, ring), `planetPixels()` pure RGBA, `planetSprite()` cached per size tier; `sky.ts` three parallax star layers + nebulae drawn once per map size; `mapfx.ts` `mapEvents()` (own plants that produced today, own deliveries today, today's gravo storm read from the log) and `createFx()` (pulse 1.2 s, spark 0.8 s, storm 3 s, at most one pulse per planet per 1.4 s, real time; the pulse uses `idleDays === 0` because efficiency after the step describes tomorrow). `starmap.ts` draws sky → storm veil → routes → planets (tier-coloured atmosphere, size grows with zoom) → effects → ships with light trails; `game.ts` feeds `map.dayEvents()` after every day and keeps drawing in pause only while `map.busy()`. Reduced motion: no pulse/spark/veil breathing, storm as steady glow. `tools/screens.ts` also shoots the map and prints the frame time with 20 AI firms at 4×
  - Own order (Anlagen, Flotte): every facility card and every cargo group of the fleet has a grip ⠿ (`data-drag`, `data-drag-item`); `drag.ts` moves it with pointer events (item follows the finger, others slide aside, page scrolls near the edges, the finger position decides the slot), `order.ts` holds the pure helpers (`applyOrder`, `moveTo`, `reorderVisible` – empty cargo groups keep their place, `parseOrder`). The order is saved with the game (`SaveData.order`, autosave and slots; older saves load with build order and tank → live); new facilities go to the end. Within a cargo group ships stay sorted by size
  - Logistikzentrale (endgame upgrade, player only): `src/sim/automation.ts` – three levels from `config.json` `automation` (2.5 / +4 / +6 M Cr for 1 / 3 / 6 Lieferrouten, needs ≥ 5 own facilities, cash and **no debt**, 1,500 Cr per active route and day), `addRoute` / `setRouteActive` / `removeRoute`, and `routesTick(w)` in `step()` right before the AI. A route = own plant → own plant, one good, a fixed Linienschiff (`lineRoute(w, shipId)` in `world.ts`; `assignShip(…, viaRoute)` and `sellShip` refuse it otherwise), amount per trip and a refill limit (`amount + refillBelow ≤` target storage, `amount ≤` hold and source storage; defaults: refill below half, amount fills the other half). A trip starts when the ship is idle and not critical, `available(src) ≥ amount`, the target has room and `stock + incoming < refillBelow`; before every trip the Linienschiff is serviced when its condition is below the route's service limit (`serviceBelow`, 35–90 %, default 60, changeable per route via „Wartung…“): on Terra at the yard price (level I: an idle ship elsewhere is sent to Terra, marked for repair), from Logistikzentrale II on the spot at source or target for 150 % (`onsiteServiceFactor`); no cash → the route waits. A waiting Linienschiff flies back to the source (ship phase `toHome`) so the next trip starts there; the daily cost is paid every active day, also during trips; `ordersTick` keeps demands a route still waits for; it calls `postDemand(…, ownSupplier)` → `postTransportForDemand(…, 'own')` → `assignShip`. Own trips carry no late penalty. `route.status` (German) says what it waits for; `trips`/`delivered` count finished trips. UI: Dynacore card in Terra, „Lieferrouten“ section and header chip in Aufträge, `routeSheet` in `sheets.ts`, gold badge on Linienschiffe, gold route lines and trails on the map, fanfare cue `automation`. `play.ts` `plannableShips()` keeps Linienschiffe out of every player ship choice. Old saves: no `routes` / `automation` = none
  - `overview.ts` Gesamtübersicht (◎ button next to the map zoom): six places in a circle (up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom; generations side by side) around the accelerator, tap for the module or what fills the place; `finaleHtml` after the sixth quark. „Welt abschließen…“ in the quark card (Statistik) and the overview opens `closeWorldSheet` (pick a quark, then confirm); `game.ts` switches to the next world with `begin()`
  - Music per world: `Track.group` (1 = world 1, 2 = from world 2: Iron Bullet Clash (Dubstep), Iron Bullet Clash (Dubstep, Jazzy RnB Cover), Is It Worth), setting `choice` 'world' | 'world1' | 'all' (`playlist`), `setMusicWorld` in `begin()`
  - `debug.ts` + `observe.html` – observation page where 20 AI companies play alone
- `tools/simulate.ts` – headless run: `npm run sim -- <days> <seed> <difficulty>`
- `tools/balance.ts` – balancing report against the target values: `npm run balance -- <days> <seeds,comma> <difficulty>` (default 1000 days, seeds 1,2,3, med)

## Key game rules (as implemented)

- One facility type per planet; extending = bringing another unit of the same type to the same planet. Max 10 sites per company.
- Facilities are bought at Terra and must be flown out as piece goods; then 5 days construction; maintenance costs from built, operating costs while running.
- Every ship carries one cargo type (tank, bulk, piece, cooled, live); amount must fit the hold. Units shown to the player follow the cargo (`CARGO_UNIT`): tank in litres (l), bulk in tonnes (t), piece/cooled/live in FE; internally all amounts are plain numbers.
- Several transports with the same pickup and destination can share one trip: `assignShip` on a ship that is still flying to the pickup or loading adds the transport to `ship.joined` if it fits (`canJoin`, `shipRoom`, `shipLoads`). All loads are loaded together, delivered and paid one by one. Loading takes `loadingDays` (2) or, from `largeHold` (40,000) capacity, `loadingDaysLargeHold` (3) days – time to add further loads without pausing; unloading `unloadDays` (1); deadlines include `handlingDays()`.
- Folgeauftrag (player only): a ship that is free within `ships.queueDays` (5) – delivering (`toDelivery` incl. unloading) or in the yard – can book one next transport (`Ship.next`, `daysUntilFree`, `canQueue`, `queueTransport`, `unqueueTransport` in `actions.ts`; `startQueued` in `shipsTick` once it is idle). `play.ts` `sendShip` picks assign or queue, `soonFreeShips` feeds the ship choices („frei in n Tagen“), the fleet card shows „Danach: …“ with „Lösen“. The AI never queues.
- Ships are never scrapped. Below `ships.criticalBelow` (30 %) a ship flies at `criticalSpeedFactor` (30 %) of its speed, logs a warning once, takes no new orders and blinks red on the map and in the fleet tab until it is repaired on Terra.
- Demand flow: requester posts demand → supplier accepts (stock gets reserved; accepting more than is on stock is allowed as an entrepreneurial risk – the ship waits at the plant and a missed deadline costs a penalty – but the amount must fit the plant's storage) → supplier posts own or public transports (a demand can be split over several ships via `postTransportForDemand(…, amount)`; `shipped`/`delivered` track the parts, each part is paid on arrival) → ship loads at supplier, unloads at requester → requester pays goods, issuer pays carrier fee.
- Terra buys final goods always and other goods as base demand; now and then (`terra.bigOrderChance`) it places a big order, marked `Demand.big`; the cheaper a good, the more Terra buys (price stabiliser).
- Order size: at most `orders.maxAmount` (15,000 FE) per demand, or the capacity of the biggest own ship of that cargo (`maxOrderAmount`). Terra orders 20–30 days of one plant's output, sometimes a big order (×2–4), never more than the biggest ship of the cargo.
- „Alle Lieferungen“ in „Transport organisieren…“ (`shipAll`, `bundleCandidates` in `play.ts`, `Sheet.alt`): ships this delivery on the ticked ships, then every other accepted delivery of the same route (same pickup planet, destination, cargo) as a whole load while it fits, earliest deadline first. Likewise „Alle Anlagen mitnehmen“ in the transport dialog of a facility/storage bought at Dynacore (`moveAll`, `assetBundle`): every waiting asset for the same planet goes on the chosen ship while it fits.
- Terra orders for final goods grow with the biggest producer: `terraOrderScale(w, good)` = most built units of that good in one plant (one planet, ≥ 1); amount × scale, order chance ÷ scale, so Terra buys as much as before in bigger orders.
- Story events (`World.stories`, off by default in `createWorld`, on in `newGame` via the start-screen switch, old saves get them through `ensureStories`): demand factor × chance and open-order cap, special price on new orders, `Demand.story` marks orders caused by an event. Planets order themselves as `world:<planetId>` (`isOutsideBuyer`, `buyerName` in `world.ts`: paid from outside like Terra, „Bevölkerung von …“); planet orders grow with `terraOrderScale` only for final goods. UI: „Lage in dieser Region“ card in Terra (`storyCard`), „Ereignis“ chip at the exchange, amber halo on the map (`storyPlanets`), toast „Neue Lage“.
- Ships are bought in the shipyard only; the delivery dialogs offer own idle ships, own ships already flying the same route ("zuladen") or public posting (exception: flying a newly bought facility/storage). A ship can be marked `repairAtTerra` and is repaired automatically when it stands idle on Terra.
- An order from an own plant (Eigen-Nachfrage) may be placed before the goods exist, like accepting a demand early; the amount must fit the supplier's storage.
- AI: prefers other companies' demands over Terra's base demand for intermediate goods (score × 0.5 for Terra), and withdraws an own order nobody took within 12 days, then orders the minimum amount – both keep long chains (chemicals → fertilizer → agro → food) from starving.
- Late deliveries: no payment when the deadline passes (reputation −3, message), but on arrival the buyer deducts `latePenaltyRate` = difficulty `penaltyRate` + `orders.latePenaltyPerDay` (2 %) per further day, at most `orders.latePenaltyMax` (50 %); own orders and own projects are exempt. Late public transports keep the old one-off penalty. An overdue delivery between companies that has not arrived `orders.overdueCancelDays` (30) days after its deadline is cancelled (`dropOverdue` in `ordersTick`; Terra and planet orders are not, they still arrive at a cut price): reservation freed, unloaded transports dropped, the supplier pays the buyer `orders.overdueCompensation` (20 %) of the missing goods' value and loses reputation. The player as buyer can cancel earlier with „Stornieren“ (`cancelOverdue`, `canCancelOverdue`; not while the goods are loaded on a ship).
- AI freight business (`buyShipsForFreight`, `config.json` `aiFreight`): when ≥ 3 public transports of one cargo have waited ≥ 10 days, a company with spare cash buys a ship of that cargo (max 2 per cargo) – keeps livestock and food from starving when ranch owners cannot afford a freighter.
- Difficulty tightens credit and interest for the player only; AI reaction speed, deadlines and penalties apply to everyone.
